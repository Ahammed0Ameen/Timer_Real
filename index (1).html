How to actually use this

1. Pick one subject, write 2–3 specific tasks. "Physics: Q1–Q5" beats "Physics."

2. Run one 25-minute Focus session, no phone. Sound helps for repetitive work (problem sets, revision) — skip it for writing or new reading.

3. Take the break away from a screen. Five minutes of scrolling is still mental load. Five minutes of standing up isn't.

4. Check off what you finished. A short, honest task list beats a long guilty one.

5. Missed a day? Just come back. The streak is a nudge, not a scoreboard — one skipped day barely matters over weeks.

That's the whole method. Everything else on this site just makes the loop easier to stay in.
